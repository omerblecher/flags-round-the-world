Flags Around the World

Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated: May 2025

COPPA & Google Play Families Policy Compliant

Our Commitment

Flags Around the World is an educational game designed for children aged 8 and up. We take children's privacy extremely seriously. This policy explains exactly what data we collect (very little) and how we use it (only to make the game work).

We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. The game works completely offline — no account, no sign-in, and no internet connection required.

What We Collect

Flags Around the World stores the following data locally on your device only. None of it ever leaves the device:

  • High scores — your best score per game mode, stored to show personal bests.
  • Mute preference — whether you have silenced in-game audio.
  • Tutorial state — whether you have seen the first-launch tutorial, so it is not shown again.
  • Session snapshot — a temporary record of an in-progress game so it can be resumed after the app is closed. This is cleared when the game ends.

We do not collect names, email addresses, location, device identifiers, contacts, photos, microphone input, payment information, or any other personal data.

Advertising

Future versions of the app may include non-personalised banner and interstitial advertisements served by Google AdMob. When ads are present:

  • Child-directed treatment is enabled on every ad request (tagForChildDirectedTreatment=true).
  • The Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) is blocked at the manifest level — it is never read or transmitted.
  • Ads are contextual only. No behavioural or personalised advertising is used.
  • Interstitial ads appear only on the game-complete screen, never mid-round or at app launch.
  • No ads appear on the pause screen.

Google's privacy policy for AdMob is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

Flags Around the World does not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or any third-party analytics SDK. App stability data is monitored exclusively through Android Vitals in the Google Play Console, which does not require any SDK in the app and collects no personal identifiers.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user data with any third party for any purpose. The only external service the app may contact in future versions is Google AdMob (see the Advertising section above).

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

This app is directed at children under 13 and is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected from any user, including children.
  • No account or parental consent is required to play.
  • No social features, in-app purchases, or links to external websites aimed at children are present.
  • Any future ad provider is required to serve child-directed ads only.

Data Retention & Deletion

All data (scores, preferences, session snapshot) is stored in the app's private storage on your device. You can delete all of it at any time by:

  • Going to Settings → Apps → Flags Around the World → Clear Data on Android, or
  • Uninstalling the app.

Because no data is held on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete remotely.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review it periodically.
