## Bugs Found During SC4 Testing

### Bug 1: Can't drag the flag unless zoomed in
- **Symptom:** At low/default zoom, drag gesture doesn't start. Zooming in first makes it work.
- **Suspected cause:** `InteractiveViewer` with `constrained: false` may be consuming the pan gesture before the `Draggable` gets it. The flag tray IS outside the IV (bottom of Column), but Flutter gesture disambiguation might be misrouting.
- **Files to check:** `lib/features/game/flag_tray.dart` (Draggable setup), `lib/features/map/map_screen.dart` (Column layout, InteractiveViewer config).
- **Potential fix:** Use `LongPressDraggable` instead of `Draggable` (requires explicit hold), OR add `dragStartBehavior: DragStartBehavior.down` to the IV to consume pans faster and not block the tray. OR verify the FlagTray's Draggable isn't being blocked by the AnimatedSwitcher's SlideTransition.

### Bug 2: San Marino (SM) hit detection still fails
- **Symptom:** Even with the MK-class fix (expanded bbox in primary pass), dropping the SM flag on San Marino doesn't register.
- **Suspected cause:** SM's path in `assets/map/world_map_paths.json` might be a degenerate rectangle shape rather than a polygon. Check `sm` bbox dimensions. Also check: is the expanded bbox being computed correctly for SM? Is the game sequence even showing SM at the right point, or is state mismatched?
- **Fix from last session:** `_primaryContains` now checks expanded bbox for micro-states (diagonal < 32). Verify SM's diagonal in the JSON.
- **Files to check:** `assets/map/world_map_paths.json` (SM entry), `lib/features/map/hit_detection.dart`.

### Bug 3: Micro-states render as rectangles (SM, VA, LI)
- **Symptom:** San Marino, Vatican, Liechtenstein appear as small colored rectangles on the map, not their geographic shapes. Screenshot: `c:\temp\falgsaroundtheworld\Screenshot_1779959246.png`.
- **Root cause:** Their `paths` in `world_map_paths.json` are simple rectangles (the SVG → JSON pipeline simplified them due to their tiny size). The `WorldMapPainter` draws `country.paths` directly, so it renders rectangles.
- **Fix options:**
  1. Update the Python pipeline to use a lower simplification threshold for tiny countries.
  2. Replace the micro-state paths with accurate polygon data (Natural Earth has better detail for small countries at 1:10m scale).
  3. For hit detection only: the expanded bbox already helps. For visuals, consider drawing a "marker dot" instead of a path fill for countries with bbox diagonal < 32 scene units, so they appear as visible dots rather than invisible or weird rectangles.
